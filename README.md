## What is BioFarm Advisor?

BioFarm Advisor is a free, open-access decision-support tool that helps farmers and researchers identify the most suitable Plant Growth-Promoting Rhizobacteria (PGPR) biofertilizer strains for specific crops, climate zones, and soil stress conditions — based exclusively on peer-reviewed published scientific literature.

---

## Key Features

- 🌾 **45+ crops covered** across major food, cash, and vegetable categories
- 🦠 **42 validated PGPR strains** with documented crop–climate–stress evidence
- 📚 **50 verified references** from NCBI, Elsevier, Springer Nature, Frontiers in Microbiology, PLOS ONE, and other indexed journals
- 🌍 **4 climate zones** — Temperate, Tropical, Arid/Semi-Arid, Mediterranean
- 👨‍🌾 **Farmer Mode** — plain language recommendations for practical field use
- 🔬 **Researcher Mode** — full technical strain details, mechanisms, and references
- 💻 **Works offline** — no login, no installation, no cost

---

## How to Use

1. Open the [live tool](https://saherkhalid99-ops.github.io/Biofarm-advisor/biofarm-advisor.html)
2. Select your crop
3. Choose your climate zone
4. Toggle between Farmer Mode or Researcher Mode
5. Get science-backed PGPR recommendations instantly

---

## Scientific Basis

All strain–crop–climate matches reflect documented experimental and field trial evidence from peer-reviewed literature covering data from over 50 countries. This tool is a knowledge translation resource — not a guarantee of specific field outcomes. Always test on a small plot before large-scale application.
